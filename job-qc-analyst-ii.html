About the role

A commercial-stage biologics manufacturer needs additional QC capacity as they scale release testing. This is a bench role on a stable team with a track record of extending contractors well past the original term.

What you will be doing

  • Perform release and stability testing per approved methods
  • Document results in LIMS with full GDP compliance
  • Support method transfer and qualification activities
  • Flag and escalate out-of-specification results
  • Maintain instrument calibration and lab readiness

What we are looking for

  • BS in Chemistry, Biology, Biochemistry, or related field
  • 2+ years hands-on QC testing in a GMP lab
  • HPLC and UV-Vis experience required
  • LIMS documentation experience
  • Comfortable in a fast-paced release testing environment
