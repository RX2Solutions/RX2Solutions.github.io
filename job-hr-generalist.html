About the role

Our client is a growing services organization whose HR function has outgrown one person. This is a true generalist seat with room to specialize once the foundation is in place.

What you will be doing

  • Run full-cycle onboarding and offboarding
  • Serve as first point of contact for employee relations matters
  • Administer benefits enrollment and annual open enrollment
  • Maintain HRIS data integrity and standard reporting
  • Support policy documentation and handbook updates

What we are looking for

  • 3+ years as an HR Generalist or Coordinator
  • Working knowledge of FMLA, ADA, and multi-state wage and hour rules
  • HRIS experience, Paylocity or ADP preferred
  • Comfortable handling sensitive conversations independently
  • PHR or SHRM-CP a plus

Working with RX2

You will work with a recruiter who has filled this kind of role before, not a coordinator reading from a script. We will talk through the opportunity with you before submitting you anywhere, keep you updated as things move, and stay in touch after you start.
