## The Situation

The client believed they needed one additional resource to push work forward, a short-term, task-focused role meant to relieve pressure on a project team. During intake, patterns emerged:

- priorities were inconsistent
- teams were not aligned
- communication was fragmented
- execution was stalling despite adequate headcount
- decision-making was unclear and reactive

Adding another contributor would have addressed symptoms, not the root cause. The organization needed leadership, not more capacity.

What they needed was someone who could establish direction, bridge communication gaps, create cohesion, and drive decisive progress.

## What We Did

RX2 shifted the engagement from "fill this role" to "solve this problem."

### 1. Elevated The Intake Conversation

We asked questions that went beyond the job description:

- What outcomes are stalled?
- Where is alignment breaking down?
- Who owns the work today?
- What decisions are not getting made?
- What are teams unclear about?

That surfaced the leadership gap.

### 2. Defined The Real Success Criteria

We partnered with stakeholders to define the leader who could create clarity, align priorities, strengthen cross-functional execution, reduce rework, and increase velocity.

### 3. Conducted A Leadership-Focused Search

We targeted professionals with experience stabilizing teams, strong coordination skills, and proven leadership in complex, fast-paced environments.

### 4. Delivered The Leader Who Could Unify And Accelerate

We presented a candidate who brought steady direction, clarified expectations, improved collaboration, reduced friction, and helped the organization regain execution discipline.

### 5. Supported Integration For Early Momentum

RX2 remained engaged to help set clear onboarding goals, align stakeholders, establish early wins, and ensure communication channels were working.

## The Outcome

The organization experienced meaningful improvements:

- clearer decision-making
- strengthened collaboration
- aligned priorities
- regained operational momentum
- reduced frustration among cross-functional teams

Executives quickly recognized that RX2 did not simply fill the role they requested. We identified the issue they **did not realize they had** and delivered the leader who could fix it.

## Strategic Impact

This case illustrates RX2's ability to diagnose the real underlying need, not just the stated role, and deliver leadership that materially improves execution, clarity, and collaboration.
