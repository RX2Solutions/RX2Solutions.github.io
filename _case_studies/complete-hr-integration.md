## The Situation

The client was expanding quickly but had no internal HR department to support that growth. Founders and operational leaders were spending time on:

- hiring
- onboarding
- compliance tasks
- employee questions
- compensation decisions
- administrative HR issues
- technology systems

This created strain, slowed execution, and introduced compliance risk. The leadership team recognized the need for HR support and chose an approach that allowed them to scale thoughtfully.

Their priorities were clear:

- keep the organization compliant
- create a better employee experience
- improve recruiting outcomes
- build scalable frameworks for future growth
- minimize distractions for scientific leadership

They needed HR to work **smoothly in the background** without requiring oversight.

## What We Did

RX2 stepped in as their **fully integrated HR department**, delivering structure, execution, and leadership across all HR disciplines.

### 1. Recruiting And Talent Acquisition

We managed the hiring process end to end, including sourcing, screening, interview coordination, offer management, and stakeholder communication. That created a consistent, professional candidate experience and reduced friction for internal leaders.

### 2. Onboarding And Employee Experience

We built an onboarding process that created clarity from day one, ensured compliance, aligned expectations, and reduced early-turnover risk.

### 3. Payroll Coordination

We ensured every pay cycle was accurate, timely, and aligned with benefits, start dates, role changes, and multi-state tax requirements.

### 4. Compliance And Risk Reduction

We implemented the HR documentation, policies, and process discipline needed to protect the organization during rapid growth.

### 5. Headcount Planning And Workforce Strategy

We worked directly with leadership to forecast staffing needs, model growth scenarios, and plan hiring cycles.

### 6. Compensation Structure And Salary Scaling

We developed salary bands, leveling frameworks, and compensation guidelines to support fairness and long-term planning.

### 7. Daily HR Leadership And Support

We handled employee relations, policy interpretation, benefit coordination, process guidance, and the daily HR decisions that consume internal bandwidth.

## The Outcome

The organization gained an HR department that:

- ran smoothly without requiring oversight
- scaled with the business
- protected the company from compliance risk
- improved recruiting outcomes
- strengthened employee experience
- clarified compensation and leveling
- reduced operational friction

Leadership reclaimed valuable time to focus on scientific and operational priorities while the people side of the business was handled at a high level. The client consistently shared that RX2 delivered more value than they expected from an outsourced partner.

## Strategic Impact

This case shows how RX2 delivers **end-to-end HR operations** for growing organizations, providing clarity, structure, and execution without the overhead of building an internal HR department too early.
