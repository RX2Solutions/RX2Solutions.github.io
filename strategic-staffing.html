Engagement Types

Three ways to bring in talent. One standard of quality.

The right structure depends on your timeline, your budget, and how certain you are about long-term need. We help you pick the fit, and every option runs through the same vetting process.

Contract

Skilled professionals on our payroll working your projects. You get speed and flexibility without adding permanent headcount, and we handle onboarding, payroll, and compliance.

Best when: project surges, backfills, validation pushes, or a headcount freeze.

Contract-to-Hire

Work with someone before you commit. The candidate starts on contract, proves the fit inside your operation, and converts to your team when both sides are confident.

Best when: the role is permanent but you want to see performance first.

Direct Hire

A full search for a permanent addition to your team. We source, screen, and present only candidates who match the skill set and the culture, so interview cycles stay short.

Best when: you know the need is long-term and want it filled right the first time.

How an Engagement Runs

From intake call to first day, you always know what happens next.

No black box. This is the actual rhythm of a Strategic Staffing engagement with RX2.

Day 1

Intake

A working session on the role, the team, the environment, and what success looks like. We learn your operation, not just your job description.

Days 2 to 5

Aligned Shortlist

A small, vetted slate of candidates who match the skills and want the job. Most are presented exclusively to you. Every submittal is one we stand behind.

Week 1 to 2

Interviews & Decision

We coordinate scheduling, prep both sides, and keep momentum so strong candidates don't slip away to competing offers.

Start & Beyond

Onboarding & Check-Ins

We manage the paperwork and stay engaged after day one, with regular check-ins so small issues get solved while they are still small.
