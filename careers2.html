The Candidate Experience

What it's like to work with an RX2 recruiter.

Most people have had a bad experience with a staffing firm. A resume goes into a void, or it gets fired at ten companies without permission. That is not how we work, and here is exactly what happens instead.

First Contact

A conversation, not a screen

We talk about what you actually want next, what you are walking away from, and what would make a move worth it. If we do not have the right seat, we will tell you that on the first call.

Before Submittal

You approve every submission

Your resume does not go anywhere without your say-so. You will know the company, the manager, the pay, and the reason we think it fits before we send anything.

Through Interviews

Interview guidance at every step

We help you get ready for each conversation and coordinate the logistics so you can focus on the interview itself. As the process moves, we share what we learn as soon as we learn it.

After You Start

We do not disappear

We check in once you are on site. Small issues get solved while they are still small, and we stay a resource for the rest of your career, not just this placement.

Our Commitments

How we work with candidates.

Four commitments that shape every search we run with you.

Your name moves only with your permission

You approve every submission before it happens. Your name is yours, and it travels on your terms.

Your time goes to real fits

We give you our straight read on the role, the pay, and the environment, so every conversation you have is one worth having.

A candidate experience we stand behind

Our goal is that you always know where things stand. We work to close every loop and pass along what we learn as soon as we learn it.

You get the full picture of every job

The whole story up front, including the demanding parts, so day thirty feels exactly like what you signed up for.
