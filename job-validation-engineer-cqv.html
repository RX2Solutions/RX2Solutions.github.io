About the role

A large biopharmaceutical manufacturer is bringing new suites online and needs CQV engineers who can work independently against an aggressive schedule.

What you will be doing

  • Author and execute IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols
  • Support commissioning and turnover package review
  • Manage deviations and discrepancies through closure
  • Coordinate with vendors during FAT and SAT
  • Maintain validation documentation to audit readiness

What we are looking for

  • BS in Engineering or a life science discipline
  • 4+ years CQV experience in pharma or biotech
  • Protocol authoring and execution experience
  • Familiarity with GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11
  • Capital project experience preferred
