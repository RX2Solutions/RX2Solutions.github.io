Leadership

Managing Partners

Both founders stay involved in client relationships and still work desks. That's not a marketing line, it's how the firm is structured.

RG

Robert Graham

Managing Partner & Co-Founder

Robert believes people are the true technology. For twenty years he has helped companies in technology, manufacturing, and life sciences unlock their greatest advantage: their people.

His approach blends innovation with intuition, using technology to amplify the human touch, never to replace it. He is a builder of teams, cultures, and momentum, and an entrepreneur at heart who knows great organizations are not managed into existence; they are designed.

Away from work, he focuses on what fuels him most: family, the ocean, and time behind the wheel chasing speed and clarity.

RR

Ross Rosner

Managing Partner & Co-Founder

Ross is Philadelphia through and through, a four-for-four Philly sports fan who followed his father and three grandparents to Temple University for his MBA.

He co-founded RX2 to build a Respectfully Professional company, one that values quality over quantity and relationships over transactions. Ross brings ten years of recruiting experience and nearly twenty years in business development, with customer service at the center of both.

Outside of work, he enjoys time with family, fishing, golfing, and hunting down his next retail arbitrage find.

Recruiting Team

The people running the searches.

BB

Brian Brown

Recruiting Manager

Brian earned his bachelor's degree in sports management from North Carolina State University before being selected in the ninth round of the MLB Draft. He spent two seasons competing in the Boston Red Sox minor league system, where he learned what preparation and teamwork really take.

He brings roughly five years of recruiting experience and is known for building lasting relationships and sharp attention to detail.

JR

Josh Roby

Director of Strategic Staffing

Josh brings ten years of progressive staffing experience, most of it in manufacturing and engineering. As Director of Strategic Staffing, he pairs deep industry knowledge with an extensive background in staff augmentation to deliver talent acquisition strategies built around each client's needs.

At his core, Josh is a businessman with strong moral values who puts the client and candidate first. He graduated from Penn State University with a bachelor's degree in business.

Outside the office, he enjoys playing sports, working out, and spending time with his family.

BC

Brandon Caruso

Manager, Strategic Staffing

Brandon has spent a decade in staffing, focused on manufacturing and engineering. His staff augmentation background shapes how he works: listen first, understand the role, then build a talent strategy that fits.

He holds himself to a simple standard, do right by the client and the candidate in every search. Brandon earned his bachelor's degree in business from Penn State University.

When he is not recruiting, you will find him on a field, in the gym, or with his family.
