Accounting & Finance

The people who keep the numbers right.

Accounting and finance is one of the hardest places to hire well. A controller who cannot close cleanly, or an analyst whose numbers do not hold up, creates problems that surface months later at the worst possible time.

We place finance talent from Big 4 backgrounds through FP&A, cost accounting, and controllership, for companies where the reporting has to be right the first time.

Every search gets the same structured evaluation and market insight we bring to scientific and technical roles. You get professionals who bring reliability and clarity where accuracy is not optional.

The roles we fill in this space.

Accounting Operations

Staff and senior accountants, AP and AR, and general ledger professionals who keep the close on schedule and the records clean.

Analysts and managers who build the models, run the forecasts, and give leadership numbers they can make decisions on.

Payroll & Treasury

Payroll managers, treasury analysts, and cash management professionals who handle the money that moves every week.
