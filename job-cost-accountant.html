About the role

A multi-site manufacturer needs a cost accountant who understands a plant floor, not just a general ledger. You will own standard cost development and month-end close for two facilities.

What you will be doing

  • Maintain standard costs and BOM accuracy across two plants
  • Own inventory valuation and reserve analysis
  • Deliver monthly variance analysis to plant leadership
  • Support annual standard cost roll and physical inventory
  • Partner with Operations on margin improvement projects

What we are looking for

  • BS in Accounting or Finance
  • 3+ years cost accounting in a manufacturing environment
  • Standard costing and variance analysis experience
  • ERP experience, SAP or Dynamics preferred
  • CMA or CMA candidate a plus
