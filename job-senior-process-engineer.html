About the role

Our client is a contract development and manufacturing organization expanding aseptic capacity at their Greater Philadelphia site. You will own process characterization and continuous improvement across two fill lines, working directly with Quality and Validation.

What you will be doing

  • Lead process characterization and optimization on aseptic fill-finish lines
  • Author and execute protocols in partnership with Validation and QA
  • Drive CAPA and deviation investigations to root cause
  • Support tech transfer for incoming client programs
  • Partner with Operations on shift-level troubleshooting

What we are looking for

  • BS in Chemical, Mechanical, or Biomedical Engineering
  • 5+ years in GMP pharmaceutical or biologics manufacturing
  • Hands-on aseptic or sterile fill-finish experience
  • Working knowledge of FDA and EU Annex 1 expectations
  • Experience authoring protocols and investigation reports

Working with RX2
