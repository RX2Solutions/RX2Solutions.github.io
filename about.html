Who We Are

Founded in 2018. Built on three decades of experience.

RX2 delivers three connected solutions: Strategic Staffing, HR Outsourcing, and Executive Search. Our leadership team has spent nearly three decades in recruiting, HR, and executive search, and that experience shows up in how every engagement runs.

We work with companies where the hiring cannot wait and the wrong person costs a lot more than an open seat.

Those companies rarely need just one kind of person. The client hiring a cloud engineer this quarter needs a quality engineer next quarter and a cost accountant after that. So we built five practices instead of one, each one added when clients started asking and each one staffed by recruiters who had already filled those roles.

Today that covers Information Technology, Engineering & Manufacturing, Scientific & Clinical, Accounting & Finance, and Professional Services, for the same kinds of companies we served on day one.

Our clients are C-suite executives, business owners, and senior leaders across technology, operations, science, and finance. What they have in common is wanting a partner they don't have to manage.
