Strategic Staffing

One Company Nationwide

A life sciences and manufacturing organization needed continuous Strategic Staffing across more than a dozen states. RX2 delivered with speed, precision, and flexibility, supporting every department and location during rapid growth.

+

A life sciences and manufacturing organization operating across more than a dozen states needed dependable Strategic Staffing during a period of rapid growth. Their work was time-sensitive and operationally critical, requiring a partner who could deliver high-volume hiring with accuracy, speed, and minimal friction.

RX2 became that partner. We supported every department and location, delivering aligned candidates in less than a week when required and adjusting quickly as needs shifted. With steady execution and consistent communication, we enabled the organization to continue operating without interruption.

Over an 18-month period, RX2 delivered 180 high-quality hires nationwide and became their sole Strategic Staffing partner.

Precision Talent Delivery

After struggling to fill a highly specialized role, a client turned to RX2 for a more accurate approach. We delivered the exact talent required without unnecessary interview cycles or misaligned candidates.

A client struggled to fill a niche, highly specialized role despite multiple prior attempts. They needed a partner who could move beyond volume sourcing and identify the exact individual who matched the technical skill set and organizational expectations.

HR Outsourcing

Complete HR Integration

A rapidly growing biotech organization brought RX2 in to operate as their full HR department, freeing leadership to focus on scientific and operational priorities.

+

A rapidly growing biotech company needed a full HR function but wasn't ready to build an internal department. They engaged RX2 to operate as their end-to-end HR partner.

We managed all core HR functions, including recruiting, onboarding, compliance, payroll coordination, headcount planning, compensation structure, and daily HR administration. That let leadership focus entirely on scientific and operational priorities while HR ran smoothly behind the scenes.

With clear structure, consistent communication, and a scalable HR framework, the organization avoided the growing pains and compliance risks common during rapid expansion.
