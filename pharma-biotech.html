Pharmaceutical & Biotech

Staffing for pharmaceutical, biotech, and CDMO operations.

This is where RX2 started, and it is still the center of gravity. Regulated manufacturing has a screening standard all its own: a candidate who looks perfect on paper and has never worked under GMP is not a near miss, they are a different profession.

We staff the whole operation, not just the lab. The validation engineer commissioning a new suite, the QC analyst running release testing, the cost accountant who understands standard costing on a plant floor, and the HR generalist keeping a growing site compliant.

One partner across all of it, so you are not managing five vendors while you scale.

What We Staff

The roles behind a compliant operation.

Across contract, contract-to-hire, and direct hire, drawn from all five of our practices.

Validation & CQV

IQ, OQ, and PQ execution, commissioning support, and capital project validation for new suites and lines.

Quality Control & Assurance

Release and stability testing, deviation and CAPA management, and the documentation discipline auditors expect.

Process & Manufacturing Engineering

Sterile fill-finish, process characterization, tech transfer, and continuous improvement in GMP environments.

Regulatory & Clinical

Regulatory affairs, clinical operations, and the professionals who keep submissions and studies on track.

Plant Finance & Accounting

Cost accountants and FP&A professionals who understand inventory valuation and variance analysis in manufacturing.

Site HR & Operations

HR generalists, administrators, and coordinators for sites growing faster than their infrastructure.
